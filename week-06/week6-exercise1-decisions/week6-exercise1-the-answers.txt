Week 6, exercise 1: Decisions (answer key)

PART 1: PUZZLES. WHAT EACH PAGE SHOWS
  Puzzle 1: Cool. 18 is not greater than 20, so the condition is false and the else block runs.
  Puzzle 2: Plenty in stock. 10 is greater than or equal to 10 (the edge), so the condition is true.
  Puzzle 3: Silver. The conditions are checked from the top and the first true one wins: 55 is not 90 or more, but it is 50 or more. The check for 25 or more is also true, but it is never made.
  Puzzle 4: Bronze. The first condition, 95 >= 25, is true, so Bronze runs and the rest of the chain is skipped. 95 is also 50 or more and 90 or more, but those checks never happen. Put the highest band first.
  Puzzle 5: Discount. age >= 65 is false, but member === true is true, and || needs only one side to be true.
  Puzzle 6: Not breezy. wind > 5 is true, but wind < 10 is false for 12, and && needs both sides.
  Puzzle 7: Take an umbrella. ! turns true into false, so !raining is false and the else block runs.
  Puzzle 8: Busy Open the second door. These are two separate if statements, not a chain, so both conditions are tested and both are true. 120 is over 100 and over 50. (The space after Busy is inside its quotes.)

PART 2: THE FOUR TICKETS
  1. fix-1-library-card.html: the age 16 must be allowed, but the condition age > 16 is false for exactly 16. Change > to >=. Test at the edge: 16.
  2. fix-2-group-tickets.html: 12 is 2 or more, so the first condition is true and the chain stops there. Put the highest band first: swap the two conditions so that people >= 10 is checked before people >= 2 (and swap the messages with them).
  3. fix-3-star-rating.html: there are two separate if statements. For 7 the first writes its message, and then the second is tested on its own and writes Loved it too. Join them into one chain: change the second "if" to "} else if (stars >= 4) {" on the line of the closing brace.
  4. fix-4-fairground-ride.html: the rule needs both things, so use && instead of ||. With ||, age 10 alone is enough, and the rider gets on.
