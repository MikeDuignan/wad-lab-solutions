  Step 9: justify-content-center centres the pair across the row when there is space left, and align-items-center puts the short tile halfway down beside the tall one. At 400 the two columns stack, because col-md-5 does not apply below 768.

PART 2: shop-grid.html
  The finished page is shop-grid.html in this folder: one product across at 400, two at 800 and three at 1200, in the same order. Six products belong in one row because Bootstrap wraps the columns for you; two rows of three would fix the page to one window size, because the HTML itself would say where the lines break. row-cols-lg-3 counts items across a line (three), not units out of twelve.

  Puzzle 1: 3 on one line at every width (400, 800 and 1200): col-4 names no breakpoint and three times four is twelve.
  Puzzle 2: 2 then 1 at every width. Two columns of six fill the line and the third, Three, is alone on the second line, because col-6 applies at every width, a phone included.
  Puzzle 3: 400: 1, 1, 1. 800: 3. 1200: 3. col-md-4 starts at 768, and 800 is above it.
  Puzzle 4: 400: 1, 1, 1, 1. 800: 2, 2 (col-sm-6 starts at 576, and col-lg-3 does not start until 992). 1200: 4 on one line.
  Puzzle 5: 400: 2 per line, 4 lines. 800: 4 per line, 2 lines. 1200: 4 per line, 2 lines. row-cols-md-4 starts at 768 and nothing in the page changes it at 1200.
  Puzzle 6: At 400 each of One and Two fills its line (nothing applies below 768). At 800 One is half the width and starts a quarter of the way along, so it is centred (col-md-6 offset-md-3 is 3 empty units, 6 used, 3 empty). Two is a third of the width and sits at the right edge, with eight empty units before it.

PART 4: THE FOUR TICKETS
  1. fix-1-no-row.html: the columns are directly in the container. Column classes only work inside a row, which supplies the flex layout, so without it each col-md-4 is an ordinary block one third wide. Put the three columns in a div with the class row.
  2. fix-2-too-many-units.html: the units are 4, 4 and 5, which is 13. Twelve is the most that fit on a line, so the third column wraps. Change col-md-5 to col-md-4.
  3. fix-3-row-cols-typo.html: the row's classes are row-col-1 and row-col-md-3. The name is row-cols, with an s. Bootstrap has no class called row-col-1, so the row has no count of items across, and the six plain col children share the line equally: all six on one line at 1200, and several to a line at 400 (how many depends on the font, because the words cannot be squeezed further). Write row row-cols-1 row-cols-md-3.
  4. fix-4-offset-sum.html: col-md-10 offset-md-2 has two empty units before the column and none after, so the notice is pushed right. Use col-md-8 offset-md-2: two empty units before and two after.
