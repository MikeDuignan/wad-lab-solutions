About us

The club was started in 2011 by four neighbours who liked walking and talking. Today we have over a hundred members, from students to people in their eighties.

Fáilte to everyone: new members are always welcome, and nobody is left behind.

What we do

  • Day walks on the second Saturday of every month
  • A winter evening walk with torches in December
  • A yearly weekend away in the mountains
